# Violets UI direction

<!-- impeccable:direction-contract -->

## Thesis

Violets is presented as a living archive of interiors and materials, not as a generic decoration landing page. The first viewport proves the work immediately and refuses a centered hero followed by interchangeable cards.

## Own world

- Mineral paper and deep pine-black ink provide the physical ground.
- Violets purple is a precise editorial marker for actions, indices, and selected states; it is never used as a gradient or glow.
- Real project photography is the primary proof and receives the largest surfaces.
- Thin rules, numbered indices, compact metadata, and measured spacing reference a studio archive without becoming decorative chrome.
- Contemporary grotesque display type and restrained body copy keep the system current and legible.

## Story

Visitors first see a real Violets project, then understand that the studio combines interior design and bespoke furniture, browse work by category, and contact the studio directly.

## First viewport

The home hero uses a compact navigation, an asymmetric text-and-image composition, a short Madeira/2011 metadata line, one primary contact action, and one gallery action. On narrow screens it becomes a clear vertical sequence: statement, actions, image, project metadata.

## Form

The homepage is a persuasive studio index. The gallery is an experience surface with editorial filtering and a focused lightbox. Contact is a direct conversion surface with studio/workshop information and email/WhatsApp actions. Components share tokens, focus states, motion rules, and responsive spacing.

## Responsive and accessibility rules

- No essential information depends on hover.
- Interactive controls have visible focus and touch targets of at least 44px.
- Menus and lightboxes expose state to assistive technology and restore focus on close.
- Reduced-motion users receive opacity/layout changes without long transforms.
- Images keep meaningful alt text and stable aspect-ratio placeholders.
